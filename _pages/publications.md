## 论文

1. **Jiulin Li**, Ping Huang, Yexin Li, Shuo Chen, Juewen Hu, Ye Tian.
   **A Unified Multi-Agent Framework for Universal Multimodal Understanding and Generation**.
   arXiv:2508.10494, 2025. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10494)
   贡献重点：参与 Any2Any 多模态理解与生成系统，将多模态理解和生成拆分为 Cognition / Deliberation 两阶段，使用角色化 MLLM agents 与 Growth-Aware Search 协调 LLM 推理和 diffusion 生成。

2. **Jiulin Li**, Mengyu Yang, Ye Tian, Lanshan Zhang, Yongchun Lu, Jice Liu, Wendong Wang.
   **WaveDN: A Wavelet-based Training-free Zero-shot Enhancement for Vision-Language Models**.
   Proceedings of the 32nd ACM International Conference on Multimedia, 2024. [link](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3664647.3681559)
   贡献重点：第一作者；从信号处理和小波变换视角提出训练无关的视觉语言模型测试时增强方法，面向零样本分类和图文检索。

3. **Jiulin Li**, Mengyu Yang, Ye Tian, et al.
   **Enhancing the Zero-shot Capability of Vision-Language Models from the Perspective of Modality Divergence / AdaDN**.
   Manuscript under review, 2025.
   贡献重点：围绕图文模态分布差异设计自适应归一化思路，根据特征熵动态调整文本端与图像端归一化强度。

4. **Jiulin Li**, Mengyu Yang, Yang Liu, Gongli Xi, Lanshan Zhang, Ye Tian.
   **A Fuzzy Error Based Fine-Tune Method for Spatio-Temporal Recognition Model**.
   Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision, 2023. [link](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-99-8429-9_8)
   贡献重点：研究 3D CNN 在低信息量视频片段上产生高置信错误预测的问题，设计 fuzzy error loss 进行微调。

5. **Jiulin Li**, Senyan Yang, Wenbo Pan, Ziwen Xu, Bo Wei.
   **Meal delivery routing optimization with order allocation strategy based on transfer stations for instant logistics services**.
   IET Intelligent Transport Systems, 2022. [link](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1049/itr2.12206)
   贡献重点：学生团队负责人；针对城市即时配送长距离跨区域订单提出订单拆分与中转站路径优化策略。

6. Jingwei Cui, Yinhan Wang, Huihuang Zhang, **Jiulin Li**, Qiaojun Wang, Lixue Yang, Hui Zhang, Qingzhe Jin, Gangcheng Wu, Xingguo Wang.
   **Process Modelling and Simulation of Key Volatile Compounds of Maillard Reaction Products Derived from Beef Tallow Residue Hydrolysate Based on Proxy Models**.
   Foods, 2022. [link](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods11192962)
